Security Architecture

Zero-knowledge.
Even we can't read your data.

  • Client-side AES-256-GCM — encrypted in browser before upload
  • Double encryption — node adds second AES-256 layer at rest
  • Noise Protocol (Ed25519) — P2P transport encryption
  • Argon2id — password hashing (brute-force resistant)
  • HMAC-SHA256 — scoped, time-limited shard access tokens
  • Reed-Solomon 4+2 — survives loss of any 2 shards

What if we get hacked?

Attacker sees encrypted ciphertext only. No keys on our servers. User data is mathematically unrecoverable without user's password.

Government data request?

We provide ciphertext only. We genuinely cannot decrypt. Zero-knowledge architecture by design, not policy.

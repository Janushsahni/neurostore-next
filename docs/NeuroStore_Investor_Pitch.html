Our Solution

NeuroStore replaces centralized cloud storage with a decentralized, AI-governed mesh network

🧬

Erasure-Coded Distribution

Files are AES-256-GCM encrypted, Reed-Solomon encoded into 20 shards (8 needed to recover), and distributed across independent nodes worldwide. No single node holds a complete file.

🤖

AI Sentinel Governance

Multi-factor reputation engine with anomaly detection, trend analysis, and churn prediction. Nodes are scored, promoted, or slashed autonomously — no human intervention required.

🛡️

Cryptographic Proofs

Proof of Spacetime daemon issues nonce-chained challenges every 60 seconds. Nodes must prove they still hold data, or face reputation penalties and collateral slashing.

🔄

Self-Healing Network

Repair daemon detects degraded objects, retrieves surviving shards, RS-decodes, re-encodes fresh parity, and redistributes — fully automated with zero downtime.
